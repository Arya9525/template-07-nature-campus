Headmistress · Primary Wing

From the Headmistress's Desk

Mrs. Catherine D'Souza (M.Ed.) on the tender, important work of the early years.

Headmistress · Primary Wing

Mrs. Catherine D'Souza

Dear Parents,

There is nothing quite like the first years of school. They are when a child learns that the world is safe enough to ask questions in, kind enough to make mistakes in, and big enough to hold every dream.

In the primary wing of St. Xavier's Academy, we care for the little ones first. Our classrooms are joyful and our corridors are safe — because a child who feels secure will dare to be curious, and a child who is curious will never stop learning.

We build the foundations of curiosity, discipline and kindness, block by patient block. Through play, stories, music and movement, every activity is chosen for holistic growth — so that when your child leaves this wing, they leave ready for anything.

Together, let's give our children the most tender beginning.

Catherine D'SouzaHeadmistress · M.Ed.
