Welcome to St. Anthony's

A Heritage of Educating Girls in Love

St. Anthony's Junior College, Agra for girls, was established in 1845 by the Religious of Jesus & Mary and is run by The Jesus and Mary Agra Educational Society. It is the vision of our Mother Foundress, St. Claudine Thevenet, to train the minds of young girls so that they may lead purposeful, positive and constructive lives.

From a humble beginning, the institution has grown steadily gaining ICSE affiliation in 1984 and sending up its first ISC batch in 1998 while living up to the homely values cherished by our foundress: education in love.

  • A Catholic girls' college for ICSE & ISC
  • Under The Jesus and Mary Agra Educational Society
  • Guided by the motto "Be a Light to Enlighten"

Our Foundress

St. Claudine Thevenet

Our Foundress, St. Claudine Thevenet, was born in Lyon, France on 30 March 1774. She witnessed the cruel execution of her brothers during the French Revolution a scene she forgave but never forgot.

Knowing that ignorance of God, a lack of love and a sense of insecurity were the main causes of demoralization, Claudine began the care and education of unwanted children. By 1818 she was chosen as Superior and became the Foundress of the Congregation of the Religious of Jesus & Mary.

  • A woman of faith, forgiveness and love
  • Foundress of the Religious of Jesus & Mary (1818)
  • "Education in love remains the best reform we can offer"
