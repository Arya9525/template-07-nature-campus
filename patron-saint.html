Missionary & Educator

A Life of Teaching and Service

Born in 1506 in Spain, Francis Xavier was a Spanish Jesuit priest and co-founder of the Society of Jesus. He is remembered as a missionary and educator who travelled across Asia, teaching and serving tirelessly wherever he went.

His work carried a simple conviction: knowledge exists to serve humanity. That conviction is the seed from which our school's philosophy grows — and why we carry his name with pride.

Why Our Patron

Knowledge Serves Humanity

St. Francis Xavier's life reminds us that knowledge is never an end in itself — it exists to serve humanity. When a student masters science or poetry or sport, they are not just learning a subject; they are taking on a responsibility to use it well.

Every morning at SXA, we begin with that reminder. Our students are encouraged to be brilliant, and to let that brilliance be gentle.
