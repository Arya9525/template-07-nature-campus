Rings of Growth

School History

Like rings in a tree, each year marks another season of growth for St. Anthony's.

1841
The Call

Six Nuns Set Out for India

A call from the Bishop of Agra, Rt. Rev. Dr. J. A. Borghi, to work for the education of young girls was joyfully accepted. Six nuns set out from Lyon, facing many dangers over land and sea.

1842
Arrival in Agra

The Nuns Reach Agra

After a long journey via Bombay, Calcutta and up the Ganges, the nuns arrived in Agra on the night of 11 November 1842.

1845
The Beginning

The Inception of St. Anthony's

The dawn of 1845 saw the inception of St. Anthony's, opening a free Day School in the Cantonment the humble beginning of today's St. Anthony's Junior College.

1902
New Premises

Moving to the Mall

In 1902 the school moved to 44 A, The Mall, and later to No. 5, The Mall (its present location), a property formerly used by the Alliance Bank.

1926
Recognition

Recognised as a Primary School

On 26 December 1926 the school received recognition as a Primary School from the Director of Public Instruction and made a steady march towards progress.

1942
Growing

Agra Becomes Central Command HQ

As Agra became the headquarters of the Central Command, the school's enrolment rose to 240 pupils, and music gained a place of prime importance in the curriculum.

1980
Girls' Only

The Marian Block & a Girls' School

The Marian Block was constructed in 1980 and, in the same year, St. Anthony's became an institution only for girls to provide education in particular to the girl child.

1984
Affiliation

ICSE Affiliation & First Batch

1984 brought good news as the institution received its affiliation from the I.C.S.E. Board, New Delhi, and sent up its first batch of I.C.S.E. students.

1998
ISC

First Batch of ISC Students

In 1998 the institution sent up its first batch of I.S.C. students, continuing its journey of quality education and the homely values cherished by our Foundress.

Toward a More Just and Humane Society

We, the sisters of the Congregation of Jesus and Mary serving the cause of education in India since 1842, commit ourselves to the promotion of a more just and humane society in our country. Aims & Objectives

The Total Development of the Student

We aim at promoting the total development of the student her adjustment to environment and her personal growth.

Adjustment to Environment

To fit each student in a modern civilized world through adjustment to her environment, we aim to train her for:

  • Home Life effective, satisfying membership of the home, wisely budgeting leisure time.
  • Work Life a suitable occupation of service to community.
  • Civic Life active democratic participation in community and nation.

"To help her leave school with principles and ideals which will guide and motivate her through life."

Explore Academics

Personal Growth

Through our education we endeavour to help the student reach a total integrated personality through her spiritual and moral, physical and intellectual, aesthetic and cultural, emotional and social formation.

In short, we help her to use the blessings of her education for the benefit of others, and to strive, influence and share in society.

From the Principal

Spiritual & Moral

To respond to God's call so that prayer and life inter-weave; to build a strong, self-reliant character of right values.

Physical

To develop and maintain a sound body, establishing habits and attitudes that are good and healthy.

Intellectual

To train the intellect to dialogue, analyse, inquire, evaluate and judge rightly.

Aesthetic

To value truth, to create, and to be sensitive to beauty in literature, art, music and nature.

Cultural

To assimilate India's culture through its conservation, transmission and renewal, and to appreciate other cultures.
