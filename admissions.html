Admissions at St. Anthony's Junior College

Admission to L.K.G. is held online, and applications for standards I–IX are made to the Principal. Admission is purely on merit.

Online Admission Fee Regulations
How Admission Works

The Admission Procedure

As published by the school in its Admission And Withdrawal notice.

1

Application

Application for admission to standards I–IX should be made to the Principal of the school.

2

Entrance Test

Pupils are, as a rule, subjected to a competitive entrance test for the standard below the one to which they seek admission.

3

Documents

Produce the leaving certificate and an official birth certificate (Municipal or Baptismal) in support of the date entered in the admission form.

4

Merit Decision

Admission is purely on merit. The Management reserves all rights of admitting any child and is not bound to give reasons.

5

L.K.G. Online

For L.K.G., registration and results are held online. The child must have completed 4 years of age.

Admission is not automatic for siblings and wards of ex-students — it is purely on merit. Donations are never accepted in connection with admissions, and recommendations will not be accepted.
Fee Regulations

How Fees Are Paid

The fee schedule is given in the fee book provided to each student. The school does not publish fee amounts on its website.

Payment by the 15th

Fees have to be paid by the 15th of the due month, else a late fee fine will be charged as given in the fee book. The whole year's fees may be paid at the beginning of the academic year.

Fee book

Bank Counter

Fees are deposited at the Syndicate Bank counter, Shazadi Mandi, Agra Cantt., which is open from the 1st to the 15th of every month. The bank will not accept fees after the 15th of the month.

Syndicate Bank

Online Payment

To promote cashless transactions and for the convenience of parents, the school has started an online payment facility, through which parents can pay school fees online.

Cashless

Dues & Compliance

No deduction is made for a broken period of attendance. Children whose fees fall into arrears for more than a month may be debarred until fees are fully paid, and Report Card or Transfer Certificate are not issued until all dues are settled.

Notice

Pay-in-Slip

When depositing fees, the three parts of the pay-in-slip should be correctly entered with the Bill No., Pupil's Name, Class and Section (in block letters), and the correct amount as given in the schedule of fees.

Careful entry

Accounts Office

For any clarification regarding fees, parents may contact the Accounts Office between 10 am and 11 am on any working day, and are requested to check their fee book before any terminal examination begins.

10–11 am

Fee amounts are not published on the school's official website. The exact fee schedule is provided in the fee book given to each student.

Documents Required

What to Bring

Documents a new pupil must produce before admission, as published by the school.

Leaving Certificate

A pupil from any recognized school cannot be admitted unless the leaving certificate is produced and countersigned by the Inspector of the State in which the school is situated (except within Uttar Pradesh).

Recognized school

Affidavit

For a pupil from an unrecognized school, an affidavit by the parent or guardian before a magistrate stating the reason for not joining a recognized school, the name of the unrecognized school, the standard and subjects studied, and any examination passed.

Unrecognized school

Birth Certificate

An official Birth Certificate — for example the Municipal or Baptismal certificate — in support of the date entered in the admission form.

Municipal / Baptismal

Moving Outside U.P.

If a pupil is joining a school outside Uttar Pradesh, the countersignature of the Inspector of Schools is necessary.

Outside U.P.

Completion of 4 Years

Children seeking admission must have completed 4 years of age, as published in the school's admission notice.

Age
Admission And Withdrawal

Withdrawing a Pupil

The withdrawal rules published by the school.

A Month's Notice

A month's notice in writing is required before a pupil is withdrawn. No notice is accepted during the school vacation.

Notice

Notice Fee

In lieu of a month's notice, a full month's fee will be charged, plus the vacation month's fee where applicable.

As per rules

Joining Outside U.P.

If a pupil is to join a school outside Uttar Pradesh, the countersignature of the Inspector of Schools is necessary.

Outside U.P.

Transfer Certificate

No Transfer Certificate is issued until all dues are paid to the school.

Dues first

Absence & Rolls

If a student is absent without leave for a minimum of 15 school days, her name is struck off the rolls, and fees are charged for that month including any intervening vacation period. This also applies to students who repeat a class.

15 school days

After Results

Parents must withdraw a pupil within 3 days of the result being declared where this applies.

3 days

Apply Online for Admission

Admission for L.K.G. is held online, and applications for standards I–IX are made to the Principal on merit. Donations are never accepted in connection with admission.
