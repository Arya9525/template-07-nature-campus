The Admission Journey

Five steps — each as gentle and guided as the campus itself.

1

Enquiry & Form

Fill the online enquiry or collect the admission form from the front office.

2

Documents

Submit birth certificate, transfer certificate and report card with the form.

3

Interaction

A gentle interaction with the child and a conversation with parents.

4

Offer & Payment

Receive the admission offer and pay the fee to confirm the seat.

5

Welcome Day

Meet the class teacher, explore the campus and begin the SXA journey.

Fee Structure 2026-27

Annual tuition in rupees, payable in two installments. No hidden costs — ever.

Level Classes Annual Tuition Caution Deposit
Pre-Primary Nursery - KG ₹ 48,000 ₹ 5,000
Primary I - V ₹ 56,000 ₹ 5,000
Middle VI - VIII ₹ 64,000 ₹ 10,000
Secondary IX - X ₹ 72,000 ₹ 10,000
Senior Secondary XI - XII ₹ 84,000 ₹ 15,000

Scholarships and sibling concessions available. Transport and hostel charges are billed separately.

Have Them Ready

Documents Required

A small checklist so your application flows without a hitch.

Birth Certificate

Original municipal birth certificate with a self-attested photocopy.

All classes

Transfer Certificate

TC from the previous school, dated within 30 days of admission.

Class II onwards

Report Card

Photocopies of report cards for the last two academic years.

Class II onwards

Passport Photos

Six recent passport-size photographs of the child.

All classes

Address Proof

Aadhaar, utility bill or passport as proof of residence.

All classes

Previous School

School leaving certificate issued by the last institution attended.
