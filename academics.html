ICSE & ISC · Est. 1845

Academics at St. Anthony's Junior College

A girls' school established in 1845 by the Religious of Jesus & Mary, affiliated to the Council for the ICSE and offering the Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) and the Indian School Certificate (ISC).

Our Academic Path

From Humble Beginnings to ICSE and ISC

St. Anthony's began in 1845 with the vision of our Mother Foundress to train the minds of young girls so they could live purposeful, positive and constructive lives.

Recognition of St. Anthony's as a primary school came on 26 December 1926, and the institution has made a steady march towards progress ever since. Music has held a place of prime importance in the curriculum, and the school was fully equipped with a full Indian orchestra by 1949.

The year 1984 brought affiliation from the I.C.S.E. Board, New Delhi, and the institution sent up its first batch of I.C.S.E. students. In 1986, St. Anthony's became a centre for the I.C.S.E. Board Examination.

Continuing its growth, St. Anthony's sent up its first batch of I.S.C. students in 1998, extending its ICSE offering to the Indian School Certificate at the senior secondary level. The school today is an institution only for girls, providing education in particular to the girl child.

Aims and Objectives

Education for Total Development

We aim at promoting the total development of the student with respect to her adjustment to her environment and her own personal growth.

Home Life

To prepare her for an effective and satisfying membership of the home, and to teach her to budget her leisure time wisely.

Adjustment

Work Life

To qualify her with a suitable occupation making a major contribution to her community in productivity, creativity, economy and fine arts.

Adjustment

Civic Life

To prepare her for active democratic participation in the life of her community and nation, in large and small ways.

Adjustment

Personal Growth

To help each student reach a total, integrated personality through her spiritual, moral, physical, intellectual, aesthetic, cultural, emotional and social formation.

Growth
Personal Growth

Formation of the Whole Person

Through our education, we endeavour to help the student reach a total, integrated personality through her formation across these dimensions.

Spiritual

To respond to God's call and achieve personal communion so that prayer and life become completely interwoven.

Formation

Moral

To develop a strong, self-reliant character that helps her make the right choice in values and behaviour.

Formation

Physical

To develop and maintain a sound body, establishing good and healthy habits and attitudes.

Formation

Intellectual

To train the intellect to dialogue, analyse, inquire, evaluate and judge rightly.

Formation

Aesthetic

To value truth, to create, and to be sensitive to human nature and to beauty in all its forms literature, art, music and nature.

Formation

Cultural

To assimilate the culture of her own country through conservation, transmission and renewal, and to appreciate other cultures.

Formation

Emotional

To increase in self-awareness, regulate her emotions, and reach natural growth by self-acceptance.

Formation

Social

To master wholesome social values and awaken to a social consciousness and spirit of ready service.
