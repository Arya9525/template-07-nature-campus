Academic Excellence, Wing by Wing

A curriculum that grows with the child — play in the early years, clubs in the middle, and streams that match ambition in senior school.

The Learning Journey

Our Wings of Learning

Explore how the academic path unfolds from the first crayon to the final board exam.

Learning begins with play. Our primary wing uses activity-based methods, phonics, numeracy and EVS, balanced with art, music and physical education. Small classes and gentle mentors make the first steps confident ones.

Curiosity gets real tools. Core subjects meet coding, robotics and dramatics, while the club system lets every child discover a passion. This is where habits of thinking take root.

Board preparation begins in earnest — with CBSE-aligned rigor, weekly assessments, a career counselling cell and value education woven throughout. Students emerge exam-ready and grounded.

Three streams — Science (PCMB), Commerce and Humanities — powered by project-based learning and mentor groups. Cambridge-aligned enrichment sharpens the senior edge.
